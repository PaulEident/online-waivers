Spring Trail Cleanup

⭐ 4.8 (42 reviews)
Environment Outdoors 3-4 hours Family Friendly

About This Event

Join us for our annual Spring Trail Cleanup! After a long winter, our trails need some TLC. We'll be clearing winter debris, repairing boardwalks, and marking trails for the summer season.

This is a great opportunity to give back to the trails you love while meeting fellow outdoor enthusiasts. All experience levels welcome - we'll provide training and tools.

What to Bring

  • ✓ Work gloves
  • ✓ Water bottle
  • ✓ Sturdy boots
  • ✓ Weather-appropriate clothing

What We Provide

  • ✓ Lunch (sandwiches, snacks, drinks)
  • ✓ Tools (rakes, shovels, pruners)
  • ✓ First aid supplies
  • ✓ Trail maps

Requirements

Waiver Required 18+ or with Guardian

All volunteers must sign a liability waiver. Minors must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.

Location & Parking

Meet at the Iron County Trailhead parking lot (123 Trail Road, Iron River, MI 49935). Free parking available. Carpooling encouraged.

Volunteer Hours

This event counts towards verified volunteer hours. Participants typically earn 3-4 hours depending on shift length. Official certificate available after check-out.
