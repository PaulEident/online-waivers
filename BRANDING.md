## Usage Guidelines

### DO:
✅ Write as "Volntir" (capital V, lowercase rest)  
✅ Use tagline to clarify: "Volntir - Volunteering, Together"  
✅ Say "on Volntir" (like "on LinkedIn")  
✅ Use "Volntir account", "Volntir profile"

### DON'T:
❌ Write as "VOLNTIR" (all caps)  
❌ Write as "volntir" (all lowercase) except in URLs  
❌ Add "app" or "platform" (it's just "Volntir")  
❌ Use apostrophe: "Volntir's" (only for possessive)
